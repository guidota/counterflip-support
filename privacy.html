Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026.

Information stored by the app

Counterflip stores game records, the last played difficulty, discovery completion, and your haptic preference on your device.

The app does not send gameplay data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking code.

Gameplay does not require an internet connection. Optional support links open outside the game.

Retention and deletion

Local records and preferences remain on your device until the app's data is removed. Deleting the app removes that local data.

Your device may include app data in system backups. Backup retention and restoration follow your device and Apple account settings.

Support messages

If you email support, you provide your email address and the contents of your message. This information is used to respond to your request.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to address your request or meet applicable legal requirements.

To ask about your support correspondence or request its deletion, email guido@tamborindeguy.com.ar.

Do not send passwords, payment details, or private keys.

App Store purchases

Apple processes App Store purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

Counterflip does not receive your payment-card details.

This website

These support pages use GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or advertising.

GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate its hosting service.

See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer at guido@tamborindeguy.com.ar with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
