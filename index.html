How can we help?

Counterflip is an offline game for iPhone and iPad. Flip tiles until every face matches.

Contact support

Email guido@tamborindeguy.com.ar.

Include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private keys.

Start a run

Tap a grid card on Home. The three cards start 3×3, 4×4, and 5×5 games.

Your first game starts with an untimed discovery board. Make all four faces match, then tap the revealed play icon.

Time and scoring

The run starts with 15 seconds. Unused time carries between rounds, up to a 30-second maximum.

Only new progress earns time. Repeated reversals earn nothing. Faster progress earns larger time rewards.

Fewer taps earn better scores. Three consecutive optimal clears unlock a partial time refund, capped at three seconds per clear.

Leave a run

Tap the exit control during play. Choose the play icon to continue or the home icon to leave.

Time continues during this confirmation. Switching to another app ends the run. Completed-round records remain saved.

Accessibility and preferences

Enable Differentiate Without Color in iOS settings to show optional face markings.

Reduce Motion removes tile rotation and the completion wash. Haptics can be changed directly on Home.

Local records

Each grid size has separate records. Counterflip does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization.

Deleting the app removes its local records and preferences. Device backups follow your Apple device settings.
